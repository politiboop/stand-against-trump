## 1. Ad spending by topic

- **Trump campaign, trans ads (CBS, Oct 15, 2024, AdImpact):** more than $19M on two ads aired nearly 55,000 times since Oct 1; MAGA Inc. more than $1.1M. Chris LaCivita: "It's the last thing on Earth they want to talk about... So we'll talk about it for them."
- **ABC (Oct 21, 2024):** Trump and GOP groups spent "more than $21 million on anti-trans and anti-LGBTQ television ads as of Oct. 9, nearly a third of roughly $66 million" (period not defined).
- **The Bulwark (Oct 24, 2024, AdImpact primary-topic coding):** $29M on trans ads in five weeks vs. $5M on the economy, the campaign's fifth topic. Since July 21, Republicans spent about $120M on trans attacks; Trump plus two super PACs almost $40M.
- **NPR (Nov 22, 2024, AdImpact):** more than $46M on "Kamala is for they/them"; $222M in GOP anti-trans and LGBTQ ads across the cycle; Democrats just under $29M on LGBTQ ads. The widely quoted "$215M" is a WaPo reporter's tweet citing AdImpact network TV only.
- **Ratios (computed, not from a source):** $46M is about 3% of the $1.4B pro-Trump presidential ad total (NBC); $222M is about 6-7% of the $3.36B pro-GOP total (may include down-ballot).
- **Wesleyan Media Project (share of airings mentioning an issue):**
  - Trump campaign Aug 26-Sep 8: economy/inflation/gas in 97.3% of 40,296 airings.
  - Trump campaign Sep 23-Oct 6: gender identity in 48.2%.
  - Trump campaign Oct 7-20: taxes 98.5%, gender identity 41.1%, economy 31.3%, immigration 27.4% of 52,323 airings.
  - MAGA Inc. Aug 26-Sep 8: immigration in 99.8% of 22,321 airings. Preserve America PAC Oct 7-20: immigration 85.8%, gender identity 78.6%.
  - Senate races Sep 1-Oct 20: GOP ads immigration 46.6%, health care 29.3%, gender identity 22.4% (292,043 airings); Democratic ads health care 36.9%, abortion 34.3%, immigration 14.4% (368,027).
  - Ted Cruz and allies: gender identity in 70% of airings.
  - 2025 summary: both candidates "placed the most emphasis on bread-and-butter issues like the economy and taxes"; pro-Trump ads focused on transgender policy "for a brief span."
- **Immigration ads (AdImpact via Immigration Hub, an advocacy group; TV in 12 states, Jan-Jul 2024):** $132.9M; GOP $117.8M (89%), Democrats $15.1M (11%).
- **Democrats on abortion (NPR, Nov 5, 2024):** "$154 million... on messaging around abortion, almost exclusively from Democratic groups, since after Super Tuesday." Harris ads Oct 7-20: abortion 32.0%, women's rights 30.4% (Wesleyan).
- **2026:** GOP advertisers "spent more than $60 million airing more than 190 TV ads that reference transgender policies since the start of the year" (CNN, Sep 27, 2026). Jan-Jun 2026: GOP immigration ads $53M across 88 races; Democrats $17M across 20 races; "Donald Trump" was both parties' top ad subject (NPR, Jul 17, 2026).

- Heritage's own voter-fraud database: 68-89 noncitizen cases since 2000 depending on the counter (WaPo via NPR; American Immigration Council). The database blocked every fetch.

## 6. Governing record, 2025-2026

**Climate.** EO 14162 (Paris) signed Jan 20, 2025; withdrawal took effect Jan 27, 2026 (UN treaty office). Memo of Jan 7, 2026 orders withdrawal from 66 organizations including the UNFCCC and IPCC. Endangerment finding repealed Feb 12, 2026 (EPA: "single largest deregulatory action in U.S. history"; vehicles only; D.C. Circuit challenges pending). Power-plant carbon limits mostly repealed Sept 14, 2026 (tracker; administration's own 2025 estimate 123M more tons CO2 in 2035). Clean-energy credits cut in the 2025 budget law, JCT estimates via Taxpayers for Common Sense: 30D EV $77.8B, 25D home $77.4B, 45Y $24.9B, 48E $165.7B, 45W $104.5B; about $507B total (computed). Biofuel, carbon-capture and nuclear credits kept or expanded. Fuel economy: about 34.9 mpg by 2031 vs 49.3 (tracker). National Climate Assessment: ~400 authors dismissed Apr 28, 2025; reports removed from federal sites by June 30, 2025. DOE canceled ~284 clean-energy grants (~$7.5B), "with one exception" in Harris-voting states (tracker). Stated policy: "It shall be the policy of my Administration to make America energy dominant" (EO 14213).

**Health care.** CBO (Oct 2025): the Medicaid provisions "will reduce the deficit by $886.8 billion" and "will increase the number of people without health insurance by 7.5 million in 2034"; work requirement alone $317.0B, +5.3M. Marketplace provisions +2.1M (CBO letter, Aug 2025). CBO July 2026: uninsured 26.6M (2025) to 37.2M (2034), "largely because of provisions of the 2025 reconciliation act." Enhanced ACA credits expired Dec 31, 2025: average payments up 58% ($113 to $178 a month), deductibles up about $1,000, enrollment 22.1M to 19.2M (KFF). House extension passed 230-196 on Jan 8, 2026 via discharge petition with 17 Republicans. Trump, Sept 10, 2024: "I have concepts of a plan. I'm not President right now." Measles (CDC, Sept 25, 2026): 285 cases in 2024; 2,289 in 2025 (3 deaths); 3,659 in 2026 through Sept 24 (2 deaths); 93% unvaccinated or unknown. Cuts against: MMR kindergarten coverage fell from 95.2% (2019-20) to 92.4% (2025-26), before and during; the U.S. has not lost elimination status (review Nov 2026); drug prices fell 3.1% year over year, mostly for other reasons per FactCheck.org.

**Education.** EO 14242 (Mar 20, 2025). Staff 4,133 to 2,183; at least 240 of 568 in the Office for Civil Rights laid off (NPR). Supreme Court stayed the injunction July 14, 2025 (McMahon v. New York, 24A1203). Programs moved to Labor, Interior and HHS (NPR, Nov 18, 2025). About $6.8B in K-12 funds held July 1, 2025, released July 25. Grad PLUS loans phased out from July 1, 2026. FY2027 request would eliminate TRIO and GEAR UP (proposal). Pocket rescission cuts $69.6M international/foreign-language education and $24.9M migrant education.

**War and foreign policy.** Iran war began Feb 28, 2026 with no authorization. War powers: House 212-219 (Mar 5), Senate 47-52 (Apr 15), House 213-214 (Apr 16); House passed nonbinding H.Con.Res. 86 (215-208, June 3) and 89 (214-208, July 23); Senate agreed to H.Con.Res. 86 50-48 (June 23; R yes Cassidy, Collins, Murkowski, Paul; Fetterman no); binding S.J.Res. 185 failed 47-50 (June 24); House H.Con.Res. 93 passed 220-204 (Sept 15, 7 R yes); Senate rejected 49-50 on Sept 24, its 14th war powers vote. Casualties: Pentagon count at least 19 dead, 11 by hostile fire (Intercept); WaPo 22 (anonymous, single outlet). CBO (Sept 15, 2026): $38.1B through Aug 1, plus $2-3B a month; "DoD did not respond to CBO's requests for information"; one-half to two-thirds of interceptor inventory used, at least five years to rebuild. USAID: 83% of programs canceled by Mar 10, 2025; closed July 1, 2025. Lancet (June 30, 2025) projects 14,051,750 additional deaths by 2030 (range 8.48M-19.66M), 4.5M children under 5, unless reversed; CGD estimates about 510,000 deaths against 2023 spending. Tariffs: Learning Resources v. Trump (Feb 20, 2026, 6-3) held IEEPA does not authorize tariffs; Trump reimposed under Section 122; a Fed staff note finds tariffs explain "the entirety of excess inflation in the core goods category."

**Equal rights.** EO 14173 revoked EO 11246 (1965, equal employment opportunity for contractors). EO 14281: "eliminate the use of disparate-impact liability in all contexts to the maximum degree possible." Transgender military ban: Supreme Court stay May 6, 2025 (24A1030); 4,240 service members with a gender-dysphoria diagnosis (CNN). Title IX: 2020 rules restored, effective Sept 29, 2026. Louisiana v. Callais decided April 29, 2026. DOJ Civil Rights Division: about 250 attorneys, ~70%, gone by May 2025 (NPR); more than 50 desegregation cases ended (DOJ, Sept 2026).
